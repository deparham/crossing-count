How to mark
  • Put a circle on every head in the picture: near the door or not, adults, children, staff.
  • Yellow circles are the current detector's guesses. Drag them onto the head, delete wrong ones (right-click, or select and press Delete), and click to add the heads it missed.
  • Make each circle about the size of the head: mouse wheel over a circle, or [ and ].
  • Nobody in the picture? Press Enter anyway: empty pictures teach it too. Blurred or broken picture: S.
  • Z undoes the last change on this picture.
