Sensor results

How far the counting system's numbers are from the verified counts
Every validation whose report was made, on this computer and in the team's shared folder, compared 15-minute interval by 15-minute interval. Only complete validations counted on clean footage are used; the others are listed below with the reason.
By traffic level
The error a system makes in a crowd is not the error it makes in a quiet hour. Each 15-minute interval is placed by its own verified crossings per camera-hour (quiet under 40, normal under 120, busy under 240, heavy from 240): comparing the levels tells crowding apart from a system that is simply off, which peak windows alone cannot.
By direction
Error = the system's count minus the verified count (positive: too many). Bias = the net error as a share of the verified count; WAPE = over- and undercounts together, which do not cancel; MAE and RMSE are per interval, in people; MAPE only over intervals with at least 10 verified crossings. 95% ranges resample whole validations (and, second, whole stores), with no range below 5.
